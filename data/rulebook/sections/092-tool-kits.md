Tool Kits
The right tools make work easy, and some jobs are impossible without them. Tool kits are 
small to mid-sized collections of tools that help you perform certain tasks. Some tools 
(fishing, makeup) give bonuses to your work. Others (alchemy, forgery) are required to 
work at all.
